Muhalli Estate & Construction Ltd.
Website Specification________________________________________
1. Strategic Foundation
Muhalli operates across three revenue streams, and the website must serve all three without confusing the visitor:
1.	Marketplace: buying and selling existing land/property
2.	Portfolio: completed construction work (houses, plazas, offices)
3.	Services: custom construction for clients who want something built
The homepage's job in the first 5 seconds is to answer: Can I buy from you? Can I build with you? Can you sell my property for me?

2. Visual Identity
Element	Direction
Palette	Deep navy or charcoal (trust, structural strength) + warm gold/terracotta accent (premium quality) + white (clean)
Typography	Bold sans-serif headings (Montserrat/Inter), clean sans-serif body text
Imagery	Real project photography only, no stock photos. Drone shots, site-to-finish comparisons, concrete/steel/glass detail shots
Motif	Subtle blueprint/grid line pattern in backgrounds to reinforce "we build," not just "we sell"
Feel	Corporate, sturdy, premium, not a cozy suburban realtor site

3. Sitemap
1.	Homepage
2.	Properties (Buy/Sell listings)
3.	Our Projects (Completed construction portfolio)
4.	Construction Services (Build for clients)
5.	Investment / Off-Plan (Land banking, pre-construction sales)
6.	About Us (Team, certifications, safety record)
7.	Blog / Resources
8.	Contact / Get a Quote

4. Homepage
Hero section
•	Headline: "Building Your Future. Securing Your Investment."
•	Sub-headline: "From residential estates to commercial plazas. Muhalli handles acquisition, development, and full-scale construction."
•	Background: optimized high-res image (not autoplay 4K video, protects load speed on mobile data)
•	Search/filter bar: Action (Buy / Build) × Property Type × Location
•	Three primary buttons: Browse Properties · View Our Projects · Request a Quote
Trust bar (below hero)
•	CAC registration number
•	Years active, projects completed, client count
Featured listings & featured projects….. mixed grid, real photos
Testimonials…. with client photos, ideally standing at their finished property

5. Properties (Buy/Sell)
•	Filterable search: location, price (₦), land size (plots/sqm), property type, status (Off-plan / Ready / Under construction)
•	Individual listing pages: photo gallery, floor plans, map, price, title/document status (C of O, Governor's Consent, Deed of Assignment, Excision)
•	Payment plan / installment info clearly stated where applicable
•	"Schedule a Viewing" and "Request Inspection" buttons
•	Sell Your Property form owners submit photos, get a valuation response within a stated timeframe
•	Real-time "Available / Reserved / Sold" status tags to avoid dead-end inquiries

6. Our Projects (Portfolio)
•	Filterable case-study gallery: residential, plazas, offices
•	Before → during → after photo progression per project (proves active building, not just marketing renders)
•	"Before & After" slider for redevelopment projects (old structure demolished → modern building delivered) strong, authentic marketing asset


7. Construction Services
•	Service breakdown: residential construction, commercial construction (plazas/offices), architectural design & project management
•	The Process, explained simply: Consultation → Design → Permits/Approvals → Foundation → Construction → Finishing → Handover
•	Get a Quote form: project type, location, budget range, timeline

8. Investment / Off-Plan
•	Off-plan pricing vs. completion pricing, clearly tabled
•	Land banking opportunities with site plan/layout images
•	Optional rental yield calculator for investors buying offices/plazas to lease out, must use realistic local rental comps

9. About Us
•	Company history, mission, values
•	Leadership and licensed engineers/architects profiles
•	Safety standards and track record

10. Contact
•	Physical office address (not a P.O. Box)
•	Phone, email, embedded map
•	WhatsApp click-to-chat with a pre-filled message, expect this to be the primary inquiry channel
•	Floating "Call Now" button on mobile

11. Blog / SEO Content
Target high-intent local search terms with practical articles:
•	"Cost of Building a Plaza in [City] in 2026"
•	"How to Verify Land Title Documents in Nigeria"
•	"The Legal Process of Buying Land in [State]"

12. Legal Footer
Include a due-diligence disclaimer, e.g.:
"All land titles are subject to verification. Muhalli Estate & Construction Ltd. acts as a developer and agent; clients are advised to conduct independent due diligence before purchase."

13. Technical Requirements
•	Mobile-first, lightweight build: majority of traffic and most property searches happen on mobile, often on limited data plans
•	Fast load times: compressed images, CDN delivery
•	Local SEO targeting city/state + property/construction keywords
•	Naira-formatted pricing (₦, "M" shorthand)

14. Technical Stack
•	Framework: Next.js 14+ (App Router)
•	Styling: Tailwind CSS
•	Language: TypeScript
•	Forms: Email Resend or Similar (For Transactional email APIQuote)
•	WhatsApp: wa.me deep links with pre-filled message text 
•	Maps: Google Maps Embed API (Office Location, property location pins)
•	Hosting: Vercel
•	Version Control: Git/GitHub

Summary: Why This Works
Feature	Why it matters
Real project photography	Proves Muhalli builds not just a listings middleman.
Title/document status on listings	Nigeria-specific trust and legal-safety signal
WhatsApp-first contact	Matches how the majority of Nigerian inquiries actually happen.
Payment plan visibility	Standard buyer expectation in the Nigerian property market
Before/after redevelopment stories	Most authentic, highest-converting marketing asset available
Legal disclaimer	Protects the company; standard practice for land dealers
Mobile-first, lightweight	Matches actual user behavior and data constraints
